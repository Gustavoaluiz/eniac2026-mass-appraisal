A Production-Oriented Machine Learning Pipeline for Real Estate Mass Appraisal with Official Geospatial Feature Engineering

Gustavo L. B. Pereira, Luís H. A. Rosa, Vinicius Braga, Vinicius Castro, Sofia Costa, Breno M. Barros, Sávio S. T. de Oliveira, Luiz M. L. Pascoal

Institute of Informatics, Federal University of Goiás (UFG) Center of Excellence in Artificial Intelligence (CEIA)

PDF (accepted manuscript) LLM prompt and neighborhood mapping

Abstract

Real estate mass appraisal underpins fair property taxation in Brazilian municipalities, but attributes that curated-dataset models rely on are unavailable in official cadastral systems at inference time. We present a production-oriented pipeline that restricts features to attributes derivable from the municipal IPTU cadastre, standardizes territory through a spatial join with official shapefiles, and uses a large language model offline to encode neighborhoods into ten locational tiers, persisted as a versioned artifact never invoked at serving time. Among three tree-based ensembles evaluated on about 35,000 deduplicated listings under stratified 5-fold cross-validation with a 90/10 split, CatBoost reaches a holdout mean absolute error near BRL 222,000 to 225,000, R2 around 0.89, and a median absolute percentage error close to 11%; coordinates with the language-model group match the full baseline using fewer features.
